## Acceptance and rollback

A candidate sample is valid only when the actual Q8 tensor readback and Metal candidate dispatch evidence are both present. The Quick screen passes only with at least 3% prefill gain beyond observed drift and without material decode, output, memory, thermal, cleanup, or identity regression. Otherwise retain evidence, disable the opt-in path, and report NO_SAFE_WIN; never promote defaults automatically.

## Review focus

- CPU flag64 remains active and identical in both arms when the Metal bit is set.
- The route is restricted to PTQ1_0/F32 M5 B8 shapes and never mutates Q8 KV tensors.
- Pipeline failure or unsupported layouts use generic Prism dispatch; no silent claim of candidate execution.
- Per-request counters reset and drain correctly; cancellation retains marker and evidence.
- The Quick workload is bounded and does not perform the long capacity prefill from Build126 Full.
